# The next app's Home shows rewards and claims them

Home in the next connected app gains a rewards and VPFI section. Its headline is what a reward claim made now would pay, worked out by running the claim itself as a preview, so the figure is exactly what the claim pays. When that preview cannot run, Home shows the protocol's own lower bound instead, labelled as a lower bound, and does not offer the claim until the exact figure can be worked out. When a claim would be refused, the reason is named: a reward day still waiting to be finalised across networks or for its funding to reach the network (with the day), the programme not started yet, the protocol paused, or the wallet flagged by the sanctions screen. "No rewards available to claim on this chain" is said only when the claim itself reports nothing to claim.

The claim runs through one shared claim flow that the Claims and Rewards pages will reuse: a receipt with the exact amount, the reward days it covers, where it is delivered (the vault, ready to use, or the wallet when the vault cannot take it; a claim never opens a vault) and that it is for this network only; the figure worked out again and the call tried right before the wallet opens; and the outcome measured from what the claim's logs record and the VPFI that actually reached the vault or the wallet in that block. A claim pays at most thirty reward days, so when more remain the finished claim offers to claim again.

The section also shows the soonest estimated removal date among the wallet's unclaimed rewards, with a final notice within ninety days of it, a statement when not every reward could be checked, and no date at all where the network removes nothing. A balance held for a wallet the sanctions screen flags is shown with its state, held while flagged or ready once the wallet is no longer flagged, and a ready balance can be claimed on its own through the same flow. The fee-discount tier is shown beside them. Lifetime claimed is left out until an index route for it exists, rather than estimated.

Rewards are described as usage rebates paid in VPFI, never as yield. The user guide's Basic and Advanced editions gain sections on Home's rewards, removal dates and held rewards, in every language.
