## Thread — a loan's activity timeline shows where its collateral moved and when its rewards were forfeited (#1566)

**Problem.** The #1566 build added three events that belong to a specific loan: a move of liquidation-fallback collateral now held in the borrower's vault, the moment an auction fill takes its collateral out of the vault, and the settlement of a forfeited reward entry. The indexer recorded all three but attached none of them to their loan, so a loan's activity timeline could never show them. Its own coverage test, which requires every loan-referencing event to be attached or deliberately left out with a reason, had been failing since the first of them landed.

**Fix.** All three now attach to their loan and appear on its timeline, each with its own label in English and in the nine translated languages. The two collateral moves are shown at low priority: each happens inside a transaction whose main action (the liquidation, the claim, the cure, the match or the auction fill) already represents it, so they sit under that row rather than replacing it. They also send no notification of their own, for the same reason. A fourth event, the sanctions stamp on a reward entry, is deliberately kept off every user-facing surface, in line with the rule that user-facing copy never mentions sanctions; the forfeit itself still shows, through its settlement. Refs #1566.
