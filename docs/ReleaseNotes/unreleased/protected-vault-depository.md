## Thread — Protected account mode and the depository vault

Every user's vault can now be used as a plain store with no loan involved. A user may deposit an ERC-20, ERC-721 or ERC-1155 asset from their own wallet and withdraw any free part of it to any address. Free means not held by a loan, offer, intent, reserve or queued payout. The tracked ledger that bounds every payout from a vault now covers all three asset kinds under one key, not ERC-20 balances alone. No payout by any route can take more than the vault's tracked holding of that asset.

Protected account mode is a new optional setting per account. It exists so that a stolen wallet key does not mean stolen funds. With it on, every payout from the vault to the owner's own wallet waits a delay of two to five days, chosen by the owner. That covers the owner's withdrawals and every protocol settlement that would pay the owner's wallet from their vault. Payments the protocol makes to the owner's wallet from elsewhere are credited to the vault instead, and payments to counterparties are never delayed. Payouts may only go to the owner or to up to five allowlisted addresses. Up to five secondary signers, each accepting from its own key, may cancel payouts, co-sign them and lock the account. A lock cancels everything queued and blocks new payouts and new positions. It never blocks loan servicing, and a settlement payout to a locked owner stays in the vault instead of failing. A majority of three or more signers may move the account to a new wallet after the delay. Every loosening change waits the delay and can be cancelled meanwhile. Position NFTs of a protected owner can move only through a queued, cancellable transfer.

An admin pause that lasts three days now opens an escape route: every user may take free assets from their own vault to their own wallet, and queued payouts keep releasing. A user may also pin their vault to its current implementation, so only they can upgrade it.

The old stuck-ERC-20 recovery, which withdrew an untracked balance straight to the wallet, is replaced by adoption. The owner declares the source of an untracked asset of any kind and signs an acknowledgement. The asset is credited to the tracked holding without moving, then leaves only through an ordinary withdrawal, so the delay covers it too. The sanctions screen of the declared source, the ban and its automatic lifting carry over unchanged.

The per-token ledger replaces the old ERC-20 balance without migrating it, so it reaches a chain only by a fresh deploy. Every in-place refresh script now refuses a Diamond from before it. Keepers gain a lock permission bit used for a lock command from the owner's linked chat. The off-chain passes that release due payouts, apply due setting changes and act on lock commands come in a separate keeper change, disarmed. Until then a due payout is released by its owner or anyone else calling the release. Each new event shows in the owner's activity feed, and a signer or nominee also sees the rows that name them.

> Caveat: the old tracked ERC-20 balances are not migrated; a chain takes the new ledger only by fresh deploy.
