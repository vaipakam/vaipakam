## Thread — transport epochs 3b-ii-A2-i: a deferred claim day is staged, not abandoned (PR #TBD)

Until this change, a claim day that more epochs listed than one read could scan was deferred with nothing drawn: the coverage the window had found was released back to the epochs, and the next attempt started over against the same live batches. Now such a day keeps what it found. The window's coverage moves into a staging record for that claimant's day — debited from the epochs, credited to no one — and anyone may continue staging the rest from where the last scan stopped, reserve the day's residual from the live sources once staged transport plus the live sources can cover it, resolve the record page by page, and be paid on the last page. Nothing settles before that last page: the day pays out, persists and exhausts its epochs only then, and until then nothing is half-paid. A record that stalls expires on a deadline sized from the work its day's list needs, and past it anyone may unwind it, returning exactly what it held; the claimant may cancel earlier. A record that has begun resolving is beyond any deadline and only completes.

Every value movement is explicit. An epoch's staged components are recorded on the epoch and named in an event with the record; the live recycled residual and, page by page, the epoch legs are held in a dedicated custody row against the record; fresh and the cap headroom are reserved by count — the live fresh row's era ledger records every debit as a spend, so fresh is never held out of it — with the figures written on the record so the unwind reverses each source exactly. Reserved headroom reads as encumbered, never as paid: a concurrent one-call settlement sees less pool, less delivered headroom and less loan-side cap, and defers rather than treating the reservation as a payout. Only an attested delivery's epoch is staged from, because the chain cannot yet tell a legacy delivery from one whose split is still in flight; the untyped population stays draw-only, as before.

To make room for this on the settle path, the claim's entry walk and the expiry sweep's walk moved off the facets that inlined them onto two hosts reached by one self-call each; the facets keep their facades and their own accounting, and the staging lifecycle has a facet of its own with its reads beside the epochs' other reads. Scope: standalone claim days. A sweep's day, a multi-chunk domain and the legacy slice are the next slice (#2305). Refs #1566, #1956, #2305, #2296; design in #2297.
