## Thread — protected mode is held by each vault (#3379 Stage B2, #3393, #3415, #3423)

Protected account mode now lives in each user's own vault, not in the platform. The vault alone records the delay, the lock, the secondary signers, the payout destinations and any pending replacement of the owner's wallet. It accepts a change only from the right key: that key's own transaction, or its signature submitted by anyone (an ordinary wallet or a contract wallet such as a Safe). Each signature names the network, the vault and the exact change, works once, expires, and can be voided before use. The rules are unchanged: two to five days of delay, up to five signers and five destinations, loosening changes that wait the delay and that any signer can cancel, and a lock that drops every pending change.

The vault enforces its own record. It pays its owner or a payout destination only while it is unlocked, and a destination only once its wait is over. An allowlisted address can verify itself (#3415); while the account has signers, a payout to an unverified address needs a signer's co-signature. Whether a payout needs a co-signature is one rule, read the same way by the release, the keeper, the app and the alpha site, so a payout never waits on a co-signature nobody can give. A lock signed by the owner or a signer can be submitted by anyone, and the keeper's lock from a linked chat goes through the vault too (#3423).

A replacement is permanent. It moves the vault's owner record to the new wallet, which from then on is paid and signs for the account, including the vault's code pin (part of #3522). The vault records that the account was replaced and never clears it: the old wallet can never be named again, the account stays protected (it cannot be switched off), and every payout, delivery and reward claim for it goes through its vault and the delay to the new wallet (into the new wallet's own vault when that wallet is protected too). Signed replacement approvals and cancels are bound to the proposal they were given for. One wallet may manage several replaced accounts, and the app and the alpha site list each of them.

Vaults are now created at an address derived from their owner (#3393), so a look-alike vault with a forged owner record is refused as a payee.

The platform reads the vault's record and applies its own side on request (anyone may ask): it holds the position NFTs, cancels queued payouts on a lock or a replacement (it counts the vault's locks, so a lock that follows an unlock it has not yet applied still counts), and raises the code-pin and NFT-lock delays. The alpha site's protection pages (safety, payout destinations, secondary signers and the known-address check) read and write the vault, report each step of a multi-step action, and on a phone scroll their buttons clear of the bottom bar. Design: `docs/DesignsAndPlans/VaultOutflowPolicyDesign.md` (Stage B2 build notes).

> Caveat: queued payout rows stay with the platform until Stage B3. A row a lock cancels is cancelled when the platform applies the lock; until then it cannot be released, because the release reads the lock from the vault.
> Caveat: until the indexer reads the vault's own events (Stage B4), the keeper finalizes only the vault changes a ledger row names, and the owner alerts for a lock or a proposed replacement do not fire. The app, the alpha site and the keeper reach the vault by direct transactions only; the signed and relayed forms reach them in Stage B4.
