## Thread — protected mode is held by each vault (#3379 Stage B2, #3393, #3415, #3423)

Protected account mode now lives in each user's own vault, not in the platform. The delay, the lock, the secondary signers, the allowlist and a pending replacement of the owner's wallet are written only by the vault. It accepts a change only from the right key: a direct call from that key, or that key's signature submitted by anyone. A signature may come from an ordinary wallet or a contract wallet such as a Safe. Each signature names the network and the vault, can be used once and expires, and a key can void one it gave out before it is used. The rules themselves are unchanged: two to five days of delay, five signers, five destinations, delayed loosening that any signer can cancel, a lock that drops every pending change, and a replacement after the delay.

The vault enforces its record itself. It pays its owner, or an allowlisted address, only while it is not locked, and pays an allowlisted address only once that address has waited its delay. A replacement moves the vault's own owner record to the new wallet, so the vault pays and checks signatures against the new wallet from then on. The vault stays the one created for the account and keeps receiving what the old wallet's positions earn.

An allowlisted address can now verify itself, by its own call or signature (#3415). When the account has signers, a payout to an address that has not verified itself needs a signer's co-signature. A lock signed by the owner or a signer can be submitted by anyone (#3423), and the keeper's lock from a linked chat goes through the vault too.

Vaults are now created at an address derived from their owner (#3393). A vault tells a genuine vault from a look-alike by that address, so a copy deployed by someone else with a forged owner record is refused as a payee.

Review fixes: each kind of signed change has its own signed type, so a signed cancel can never be used as a request or a removal. A verification must name this vault's account and platform. A second replacement drops the outgoing wallet's pending changes just as the first does, and the new wallet cannot switch co-signing off during its cool-down. Removing the last signer ends the co-sign requirement. An allowlisted address with protected mode on is paid into its own vault. The alpha site's safety card now reports every step of a multi-step action, including one that landed before a later step failed. A signed replacement cancel names the proposal it cancels, by nominee and round. Whether a queued payout needs a co-signature is now one rule, read per payout by the keeper, the app and the alpha site, so a payout to an unverified destination shows the co-sign it needs and a signerless account's payouts are released. When a lock or a switch-on lands in the vault but the platform's application does not, the alpha site reports the vault's change as done and the platform side as still to do. A replaced account stays routed through its vault to the new wallet for good: every payout, delivery and reward claim for it reads one rule, and its protected mode can no longer be switched off, so its payouts also keep their delay. Every signed replacement action is bound to the proposal round it was given for. A pending change voided by a lock, a replacement or a switch-off reads as not pending. A payout whose destination was removed is cancelled by an ordinary release, which the keeper now sends. Retirement is recorded by the vault at the first replacement and never cleared, and a replacement may not name the account's original wallet or the one holding it now. The alpha site's payout destinations, secondary signers and known-address list read and write the vault's record. A vault's pin authority follows its owner record, so after a replacement the new wallet can unpin it and the old wallet cannot (part of #3522). The platform counts the vault's locks, so a lock taken after an unlock the platform had not yet applied still cancels the payouts and changes queued since. A matured signer removal no longer expires (only additions do). A new wallet that has protected mode on in its own account receives a replaced account's payouts into its own vault. One wallet may manage several replaced accounts, and the app and the alpha site list each of them.

The platform reads the vault's record and applies its own side of it on request (anyone may ask): it holds the position NFTs and cancels queued payouts on a lock or a replacement, and raises the code-pin and NFT-lock delays. The deploy records the protection code as `vaultProtection`. Design: `docs/DesignsAndPlans/VaultOutflowPolicyDesign.md` (Stage B2 build notes).

> Caveat: queued payout rows stay with the platform until Stage B3. A row cancelled by a lock is cancelled when the platform applies the lock; until then it cannot be released, because the release reads the lock from the vault.
> Caveat: the app, the alpha site and the keeper reach the vault's entries by direct transactions only; the signed and relayed forms reach them in Stage B4.
