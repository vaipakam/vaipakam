## Thread — Everything a user is owed lands in their vault; one Withdraw reaches a wallet (#3745)

The platform now has one rule for where it pays a user (owner decision of 2026-11-19, design in `docs/DesignsAndPlans/PayoutDestinationRuleDesign.md`). What a loan, an open offer, a reservation or a lock releases goes into the entitled account's vault as free balance. Only the owner's own Withdraw, or the release of a queued protected-mode payout, takes it to a wallet. So one action behaves the same for every account, and a step someone else triggers never pushes tokens at another person's wallet or opens a queued payout on their account.

The outflow policy gains an own-balance release. It is used when the amount is already in the entitled account's vault under a lien or a reservation: Auto-lend capital taken back, a loan reserve taken back, a prior seller's interest, and a sale vehicle's collateral. The caller first drops the lien or reservation by exactly the amount. The policy then checks that the vault holds it unencumbered and credits it to the free-balance ledger. Nothing moves, so no payout queue applies, and the credit never lifts the ledger above what the vault holds unencumbered. One `VaultOutflow` event records each release, with the account's own vault as the destination.

A taken-back loan reserve is released into the depositor's own vault, not paid to the borrower position's current holder. The depositor funded it, and the reserve does not travel with a position sold since. A prior seller's interest keeps its collect step (`claimAsSeller`), now into the seller's free balance: the reservation keeps the seller's own sanctions screen at collection rather than at whoever paid. A recovered account's capital and reserve can still be released by anyone, for the recovery sweep; the separate wallet branch for that case is gone because no release reaches a wallet.

The entry names `withdrawLenderIntentCapital`, `withdrawLoanReserve` and their events are unchanged, so callers and the indexer keep working; their meaning is now "take back into free balance".

Rewards, salary and the VPFI exit follow the same rule. An interaction reward is always credited into the claimant's vault, whoever the claimant is; the `Wallet` venue is refused, and a credit that cannot complete reverts the claim so the reward stays claimable (there is no wallet fallback any more, and the vault is created if the claimant has none). A salary stream's vested amount is paid into the beneficiary's vault. The VPFI exit (`withdrawVPFIFromVault`) is now the one Withdraw for the VPFI token, with the same bound, pause rule and protected-mode queue as every other withdrawal; its name and event are unchanged.

A user can collect and withdraw in one transaction. Inside a batch (`multicall`), the free-balance ledger counts what the batch credits to the caller, per asset, less what it debits. A last item `withdrawCredited(kind, asset, tokenId)` withdraws exactly that, never more than is withdrawable now, through the ordinary Withdraw. Outside a batch it does nothing. The figure only sizes the withdrawal and grants nothing.

A new build check (`check-wallet-payouts.mjs`, pre-deploy step 1d3b) allows the two wallet doors of the outflow policy only from the one Withdraw and the protected payout queue's release, and lists every direct push of value with its reason (a liquidator's or keeper's bonus, the admin's treasury claim, a credit into a vault).
