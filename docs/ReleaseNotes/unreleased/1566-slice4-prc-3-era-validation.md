## Thread — an arriving delivery states the era it was dispatched for, and the chain holds one it cannot place; every era counts the packets still open for classification (#1566 slice 4 PR C-3)

**Problem.** A remittance in flight across a role change could land in the new era and be credited there, while the obligations it was dispatched for belonged to the era just retired — and the registry would then have no way to know that a packet still awaited classification in an era it wanted to close. The design asks the receive ingress to validate a delivery's intended era against the live one and to route a mismatch to retry or quarantine, and it asks every era to carry a count of packets whose classification is not yet final, so that an era closes only when that count reads zero.

**What changed.** The rooted wire carries the era it was dispatched for (the previous slices). A delivery for an era AHEAD of this chain's live era is refused by name: the sender has transitioned and this chain has not, and the transport re-delivers it after the local transition. A delivery for an era this chain has already RETIRED is quarantined: held whole and unclassified under its packet — no epoch is opened, nothing is credited to the live ledger or the recycle bucket, and no split is attested — until evidence classifies it through the reconciliation path, exactly as any other unclassified value. An era-less list-bearing delivery carries no era, so it is placed by the days it lists: listing only days from the live era's boundary on, it belongs to the live era and is taken as before; listing any earlier day, it is treated the same way as a retired-era delivery; listing no day, it funds the live era, since it names no obligation of a retired one. The design's drain-and-freeze rule for era-less lanes makes the quarantine the delayed case, not the common one. The compensation wire is unchanged: it is day-scoped and its day's clock already pins its origin.

**The open-classification count.** A packet is counted open in the live era the moment unclassified value is attributed under it, and counted closed exactly once, when nothing is left unclassified under it — at any of the four exits that move value out of the unclassified attribution (a transport draw, a classification, a repatriation return, a quarantine release). A packet that was final and receives unclassified value again re-opens in the era it was first counted into. Packets recorded before this slice carry no count; the history backfill assigns them. The count and the finality mark are hosted once, on the era facet, and reached by internal call from the exits, so the custody and reconciliation facets gain one call each rather than a body.

**Spec:** TokenomicsTechSpec, slice-4 bullets gain a fifth change (the intended era and the open-classification count).
