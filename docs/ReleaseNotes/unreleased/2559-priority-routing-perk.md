## Thread — the first perk with an effect: a priority credit lets a refused intent fill through (#2550, #2559)

The spend-gated perk channel could sell entitlements but nothing used one, so no perk could responsibly be priced. The first effect is now built: priority routing for lender intents. A lender who buys credits and opts one of their live intents in gets fills that would otherwise have been refused. When a solver's service capacity is used up, a fill of that intent still goes through, spending one of the lender's credits instead of the solver's capacity. When the solver can afford the fill, it is charged as usual and no credit is spent, so a credit only ever buys a fill that would not have happened. Nobody else is reordered or charged more, which keeps the rule that a perk may change what its buyer gets but never what other participants see.

The opt-in belongs to one specific intent. Rewriting the intent resets it and cancelling clears it, so a replacement never silently spends the lender's credits, and every change is recorded. Intents run by the protocol itself, the aggregator adapters and the backstop vault, cannot opt in. The perk cannot be sold in a form it cannot deliver. It can only be priced as counted credits, and it cannot be bought unless it is configured that way, the solver capacity limit is on and lender-intent matching is switched on. Taking its price down is always allowed, so a deployment that had configured it as a timed perk before it gained its effect can still withdraw that offer, and such a timed configuration is never sold again. That check is made at the purchase, so a later change that switches either off stops sales at once. A new read shows a solver, as of the current block, whether its own capacity covers a fill or the lender's credit would.

Nothing is priced. The perk stays unsold until three things are in place on a chain: the solver capacity limit is on, the keeper has been updated so it keeps looking for credit-backed intents after a capacity refusal, and the effect has been verified on a fork. The app gains its screens when lender intents get a user interface.

Closes #2559. Refs #2550.
