## Indexer — reconciliation repairs: settled-head hold, orphan withdrawal, missed settlements, holder history (PR #2636)

The indexer no longer reads the chain up to a guessed point. Each turn it asks the RPC for the chain's settled ("safe") block; when that read fails for any reason, it used to step back 32 blocks from the newest and scan up to there, and because the scan never revisits a block, a reorganisation deeper than that margin would have left wrong rows in the database for good. A turn without a settled block now holds: it scans nothing, keeps its place, keeps serving what it has, and logs which chain is held and the reason the RPC gave. The cursor's age on the ingest health page grows while it lasts. Every chain the platform indexes today answers the settled-block question, so in practice this is a one-turn pause on a timeout. Closes #2201.

A loan record the chain has no loan for is now withdrawn from publication rather than only reported. When the reconciliation pass proves, at the settled block, that a loan id does not exist on chain, it moves the record out of the loans table (together with any listing or swap commitment attached to it) into a separate holding table that keeps the whole record and the block the proof was read at, so an operator can restore it if the check was wrong. The finding stays on the operator's stale-quarantine report. Every surface that lists or counts loans stops showing it with no per-surface change. Closes #2197.

An ended loan whose settlement announcement the indexer missed entirely is now brought up to settled from the chain's own status. The settlement heal used to consider only records whose settlement event was in the activity ledger; it now checks every ended-but-unsettled record against the chain on its rotation and promotes it to settled only when the chain says Settled, with the settlement's block and time left unknown unless a confirmed event supplies them. Closes #2192.

A corrected loan's chain-verified holders are now recorded in its participation history, in the same transaction as the correction, so a holder whose transfer fell in the missed stretch appears in the wallet's loan history and Activity filter. A holder who already has an entry is left as recorded, and a side the chain did not name records nothing. The other half of #2195 (a reconciliation-marked ending in the Activity ledger) and #2196 (a durable broadcast for repairs) are in a follow-up change.
