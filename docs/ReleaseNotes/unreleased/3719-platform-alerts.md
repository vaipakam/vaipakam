# The platform's own record of every loan, offer and protection change (#3719)

A user who reaches the platform through another website sees only what that website shows. A new keeper pass now sends each user the platform's own record, built from the chain, whichever website was used. When a loan opens, both parties are told its true terms: what was lent, the collateral, the interest rate, the term, the website named as integrator, and every warning the contract derived (collateral at the concentration caution level, a rate far above or far below the recent average for its term). When an offer is posted or edited, or the rate terms of an Auto-lend slot are set, its owner gets the same facts, including the market link's margin where the rate follows the market. Anything the chain event does not carry is stated as unknown, never left out. An offer that the same transaction turned into a loan is not announced on its own, since the loan's message covers it.

The same pass warns a wallet of every step that lowers its protection: a higher asset access level, consent to lend or borrow against a pair without a market price, a keeper allowed to act for it (in general, or on a loan or an offer), a position token approved to another address, and a request to unlock its position tokens or to shorten the wait before they unlock. Each warning says when the change takes effect and to open the app if it was not them. Steps that tighten protection send nothing.

Like the vault security alerts, this pass reads the main contract's and the position token contract's events from the chain, not from the indexer, so these safety messages do not depend on it. It keeps its place by block and event, so a run cut short or a delivery that must be retried never repeats a message and never skips one; a backlog of more than 20,000 blocks is skipped and counted. Messages go through the agent's Telegram route and Push, in each user's language. The pass sends no transaction and ships disarmed under the new `PLATFORM_ALERTS_ENABLED` flag.
