## Thread — A lender can close out an overdue loan whose collateral must be sold (PR #<n>)

A lender whose borrower missed repayment and the grace period after it could already close the loan out from the position page — except in the commonest case, where the collateral is a liquid token that has not collapsed in value. The protocol only closes those with a list of exchange routes to sell the collateral through, and the app had no way to build one, so the overdue card told the lender the position was eligible and that an operator was needed. Loan 21 on Base Sepolia sat in that state for about 85 days.

The card now builds the route itself. When the lender confirms, the app reads which swap adapters the protocol has registered, asks each one it can reach for a price — the aggregators through the agent's existing key-holding quote proxy, Uniswap V3 through its on-chain quoter, and on testnets the declared mock adapter — and sends the ranked list with the close-out. The adapter positions come from the protocol at that moment rather than from a table the app keeps, so governance adding, disabling or reordering an adapter cannot misdirect a route. The protocol's own oracle-based minimum still binds every sale. The card and its confirmation now say what that route can lead to: sale proceeds in the lent asset, or, if no exchange can meet the minimum, the protocol's fallback, where the lender claims collateral and the borrower can still cure. They also say whether the caller incentive applies to the viewer's wallet. Where the protocol has no exchange the app can ask on that network, the card keeps saying an operator is needed. A confirmation that gets no usable price sends nothing.

The rules for building a route — reading the adapter list, each venue's data format, parsing aggregator responses and ranking — now live in one shared library module. The keeper's liquidation quoting uses the same formats and parsers instead of its own copies. Its hand-kept adapter indexes, its missing Base Sepolia entry and its Balancer data source remain open in #2468, along with a keeper pass that closes past-grace loans itself, as follow-ups. Closes #2463.
