## Thread — A real illiquid-collateral loan refinanced through the live app; indexer ingest restored (PR #2422)

Loan 22 on Base Sepolia was refinanced end to end through app.vaipakam.com on 5 October, using the dev test wallets. That loan is backed by illiquid collateral that both parties consented to, and it is the loan whose refinance used to fail (#2380). The borrower posted the refinance request from the loan's page (request #45, 12% for 30 days). A different lender funded it through the app's own review-and-sign steps. All five transactions succeeded, and each wallet's transaction count rose by exactly the transactions the drive saw. Their contents were later compared field by field with the requests the committed drive now expects, with no differences.

What the run checked on chain. Request #45 targeted loan 22 by carry-over, at the typed rate ceiling and length, for the old principal and collateral. Loan 22 closed as repaid, and loan 23 opened from that request for the same borrower and the accepting lender, on the same collateral and principal, still marked illiquid with both parties' consent recorded, at the requested rate and length. The borrower's balance of the collateral token never moved.

What was checked after the run, against the same blocks. These checks were added to the committed drive during review, which now makes them itself. Reading the chain at the block before the accept and at the accept block showed the collateral lien released and zeroed on loan 22 and live, with the same borrower, asset and amount, on loan 23. The same reads showed the settlement matching the contract's own payoff figure to the smallest unit. The old lender's claim and vault rose by the payoff less the 2% treasury share of its interest. The borrower received the new principal less the 0.2% initiation fee and paid the payoff. The accepting lender paid the principal and received the 1% matcher share of the fee. The transaction's own token transfers show the treasury receiving its share of the interest and of the fee. The figures on both review screens were also compared against what the app rendered during the run, and they match the chain.

What this drive does not check. It does not cover a refinance accepted inside the grace window, an exiting lender's VPFI yield-fee discount, a loan with liquid collateral, the old lender claiming the payout, how the indexer and the app's lists show the request and the new loan afterwards, or reward accounting. It also does not check whether an approval is left standing. The app approves the payoff for the request's last fillable moment, so an earlier accept leaves the difference approved; this run left about 0.0000764 WETH. The drive's verdict is now a list of the claims it verified, each with the chain reads behind it, and the claims it did not, each with where it is covered. It replaces the single "all checks passed" line. The drive is committed as a reusable live check, and a rerun against loan 22 now stops before sending anything because the loan is closed.

The drive turned up two things, each filed. First, the lender's review never says the request is a refinance: it does not mention that accepting also pays off and closes the borrower's existing loan, and it describes the carried-over collateral as newly locked (#2421). Second, the production indexer had stopped advancing at 05:35 UTC. The merge that added the offer collateral-range columns was published automatically without its database migration being applied, and the safety check added the same day correctly held all indexing until the schema caught up. The two pending migrations, both of which only add columns, were applied by hand at 09:02 UTC, and indexing resumed on its next scan with no redeploy; it was advancing again by 09:06. Until then, today's new offers and loans were missing from the app's lists. Making the automatic deploy apply migrations first, and alerting when indexing pauses, is tracked in #2420.
