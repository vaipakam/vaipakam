## Open offers — the offer's escrow and its rights follow its position token (#3744)

An open offer has its own position token, and whoever holds it when the offer fills takes the loan position. Its escrow and its rights now follow that holder too (owner decisions of 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19). Whoever holds the token cancels the offer, changes its terms, lowers its size, arms its price guard, stages it, chooses its keepers and lowers a collection offer's loans. The rule is written once and served by one host, `OfferBookFacet.requireOfferHolder`, which every facet asks. Anyone may still clear an expired offer, an offer whose route is dead, or an offer of a recovered account, and the holder's controller may cancel once a protected-account replacement retired the holder.

Everything an offer returns goes to the vault of the token's holder as free balance: a cancel, an expired-offer cleanup, a downward size edit, a collection offer's released backing, a match's unused collateral or closing remainder, and a direct accept's unused collateral. One host (`OfferCancelFacet.refundOfferEscrow`) does this for every step, and the outflow policy reads the holder from the token, so no step names a payee. While the creator still holds the token nothing moves: the escrow becomes free in the creator's own vault. Raising an offer pulls more from the creator's wallet into the creator's vault, so only the creator, still holding the token, may raise it.

A step someone else triggers is never blocked by a holder who cannot be paid, such as a sanctions-flagged wallet with no vault. The amount then stays in the creator's vault, reserved for the token so the creator cannot take it, and the token's holder collects it with `collectOfferRefund`. `OfferEscrowReturned` names the holder for every return and says whether it was held; `OfferRefundCollected` records a collection.

A caller without the right is refused with `NotOfferHolder`. A cancel of an accepted or sold offer is refused for that before it asks who is calling. The cancel refusal `NotCreatorOrNotExpired` keeps its name for the decoders that read it, but its first field is now the token's holder. The rule and its reasons are in `docs/DesignsAndPlans/PositionSaleStateGuardDesign.md` §3.10.
