## Positions — "Past grace" badge (#2463 follow-up)

A loan on the Positions page whose grace period has ended now shows a "Past grace" badge instead of the generic "Past due", so a lender can see which positions have reached the point where they may be closed out and a borrower can see which loans are past their ordinary repay window. The answer comes from the protocol itself, read for each open loan, because the grace length is set by governance and judged by the chain's clock; a device clock that runs behind cannot hide the badge, and until the protocol's answer arrives the row keeps its ordinary time-based label, and every open row carries a line saying the protocol is being asked whether grace has ended (or that it could not be asked, when the read fails or the device is offline; an earlier answer is not repeated in either case), so that label is not taken as the whole story whatever it says, since the device clock may be behind. That line sits under the row rather than inside the badge, so a health warning in the badge cannot hide it. The answer is re-read every minute while the page is in use (less often once the session goes idle, with a catch-up on the first interaction back, and not at all while the tab is hidden), including after it says grace has ended, because governance can lengthen the grace schedule and reopen repayment. A line under the row explains what it means for the viewer's side, visible on touch screens and to keyboard users rather than only on hover; it says a close-out MAY now be possible and points at the position page, because grace ending is a time fact and a close-out can still be refused for reasons the row does not read (a pause, for one). It never implies only the lender may close the loan out. Follow-up to #2463.
