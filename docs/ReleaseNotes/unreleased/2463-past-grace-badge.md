## Positions — "Past grace" badge (#2463 follow-up)

A loan on the Positions page whose grace period has ended now shows a "Past grace" badge instead of the generic "Past due", so a lender can see which positions they can close out and a borrower can see which loans can no longer be repaid the ordinary way. The answer comes from the protocol itself, read for each open loan, because the grace length is set by governance and judged by the chain's clock; a device clock that runs behind cannot hide the badge, and until the protocol's answer arrives the row keeps its ordinary time-based label. The answer is re-read every minute, including after it says grace has ended, because governance can lengthen the grace schedule and reopen repayment. A line under the row explains what it means for the viewer's side, visible on touch screens and to keyboard users rather than only on hover; it says a close-out MAY now be possible and points at the position page, because grace ending is a time fact and a close-out can still be refused for reasons the row does not read (a pause, for one). It never implies only the lender may close the loan out. Follow-up to #2463.
