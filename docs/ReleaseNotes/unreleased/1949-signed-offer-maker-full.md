## Thread — a signed-offer maker can now opt into the Full tariff on the order they sign (#1949)

**Problem.** The Full tariff opt-in was reachable for standing offers (the creator sets it on their open offer after posting) and for acceptors (on the acceptance they sign), but not for a maker who posts a gasless, signed order: that order is materialized and accepted in one transaction, so there is no moment between posting and acceptance to set anything. A signed maker therefore always resolved as non-Full — safe, nothing was mis-charged — but the Full absorption channel was unreachable from the order book. The matched-fill half of this gap closed under the earlier card; this was its untracked remainder.

**What changed.** The signed order now carries the maker's Full opt-in — the opt-in flag, the ceiling on the maker's tariff and whether a downgrade is permitted — as part of the artifact the maker signs, so it travels with the signature. When a fill materializes the order (a direct fill, a wallet-backed fill, or a keeper matching a slice of it), the opt-in is applied to the materialized offer with the same fields and the same event the standing-offer setter uses, and the acceptance charges the maker's tariff and stamps Full exactly as it would for a standing offer: the tariff a party pays does not depend on the venue that filled their offer. A Full opt-in without a ceiling is refused before any value moves, on every fill path, as the setter refuses it. A slice filled by a keeper carries its pro-rata share of the signed ceiling, rounded up, so the signed ceiling bounds the whole order and no non-zero ceiling ever rounds to nothing for a small slice. Makers who do not opt in are unchanged.

**A breaking signature shape.** Because the signed order gained fields, orders signed under the previous shape no longer verify. Pre-live, this is a book reset: the connected app, the indexer's order book and its validation, and the parity tests all moved to the new shape in the same change, and any order posted under the old shape must be re-signed. The maker's ticket does not yet offer a control for the opt-in — the fields default to off — so the control is a separate, app-side card; the signer, the wire shape and the on-chain honouring are what this card delivers.

**Spec:** the connected-app spec's Full VPFI tariff opt-in section no longer says a signed-order maker has no opt-in surface. **Plan:** M2's remaining row reads built.
