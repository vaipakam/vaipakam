## New app: key risks with the Terms and in every review

The new app now says plainly what can go wrong. When the Terms of Service are asked for, the prompt shows a short list of key risks above the accept step: funds can be lost and nothing is insured or reversible, what a lender receives if a borrower does not repay, what a borrower can lose, that the software, price feeds, tokens and networks can fail, that only you control your wallet, and that nothing here is financial advice. The list wording was approved by the owner.

Every review that opens a loan or a rental now carries one "If things go wrong" line for that side and that loan. A lender on two liquid assets is told the collateral is sold to repay them, or given to them if it cannot be sold; where either asset is illiquid the loan never closes by a sale and the collateral itself goes to the lender. A borrower is told what can cost them their collateral. A signed order, whose assets are checked again when it fills, gets a line that holds either way. No extra signature is asked: the review you sign carries the line.

A new public page, Key risks, linked from the footer and from every such line, holds the list and four more points: a pegged token can lose its value, a price feed can be wrong or late, tax and local rules are the user's own, and wallets, networks and transfers are run by others.

The list is not yet part of the published Terms text: the next Terms version, which waits for legal review and a governance change, will carry it, and every wallet will be asked to accept again then. This is #3661 and needs no contract change. The rule is in the rulebook as RB-KEY-RISKS.
