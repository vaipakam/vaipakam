## Thread — a broadcast the chain cannot route after its source rotated is parked with a recorded disposition, not bounced for ever (#1566 slice 4 PR C-4)

**Problem.** When a mirror's canonical source rotates, the older era-less broadcast wires can no longer be trusted to describe the live era, so the chain refused them — by reverting the delivery. A reverted cross-chain delivery sits in the transport's failed queue indefinitely, re-executable but never acceptable, with no record on the chain that it ever arrived and nothing an era's close-out could see. The design asks for a parked-message lane instead: accept the message, park it with its identity, let anyone retry it against the live gate, and let the operator tombstone what the gate will never accept — and let no era finalize while a broadcast parked in it stands.

**What changed.** The two refusals of era-less broadcasts (the older single-day wire and the older per-destination wire) now PARK instead of reverting: the delivery is accepted, no day state is installed, and a record is kept — the wire's kind, the day, a digest of the broadcast, the receipt time and the custody era live at receipt — counted against that era. Anyone may retry a parked broadcast by re-supplying it; the digest proves it is the one parked, and the same apply runs through the same gate, so it is applied exactly when the gate would now accept it and stays parked, by name, while the gate refuses. A broadcast first received after the first era closed is quarantine-only: it exits by tombstone alone, because the next era's opening never certifies a later arrival as belonging to it. The operator tombstones a parked broadcast with a recorded reason; that is an operator decision by design — the platform choosing to stop waiting on a message it cannot route. An era whose lane still holds a parked broadcast is not terminal, beside its two obligation counts.

**Why the retry exists although today's gate never clears.** The rotation that retires the era-less wires is permanent on this tree, so a parked legacy broadcast is never re-accepted; the lane's value today is the record and the disposition. The retry keeps the design's shape at no cost, so a gate that can clear — the era registry replacing the rotation flag as the authority, in a later slice — has its retry ready.
