## Thread — Automated delisting the Safe can veto (PR #TBD)

Delisting a wallet from the platform's own sanctions list can now be proposed by a bot instead of being prepared by hand, without taking the decision away from the Safe. A new delisting timelock is appointed as a second remover of the list. Its bot may propose only wallets the list records, against the OFAC publication the list records, and no more than that publication declared outstanding. Nothing happens for a waiting window (48 hours by default). During the window the Safe or the admin can cancel, and that cancel is a lasting veto on each wallet: the bot cannot bring a vetoed wallet back until the Safe lifts it. When the window ends, RedStone's Sanctions Oracle must also not flag the wallet. Where RedStone runs on the same chain it is read directly; elsewhere a small companion contract on a chain where RedStone runs reads it and sends the answer over Chainlink CCIP, and only a reading taken after the window and still recent counts. With no RedStone source configured, automated delisting stays closed and the Safe's own direct delist, which is unchanged, remains the only route. A new testnet deploy script installs the timelock for the recorded rehearsal list; it configures no RedStone source, so on testnets proposals and vetoes can be rehearsed while delistings themselves stay with the Safe.
