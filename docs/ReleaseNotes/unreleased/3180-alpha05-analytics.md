## Thread — alpha05: Analytics, the protocol's public figures

The new app now has its Analytics page, reached from the Advanced navigation or by its address, with no wallet. It shows the protocol's own figures on one network: how many loans are open and have ever opened, the offers in the book and ever posted, the wallets that took part, the average agreed rate and the share of loans that ended in default or liquidation. A picker lets a visitor choose any network the app serves; without a choice the page describes the wallet's network, or the default one.

Every figure is read from the protocol contract on that network at one block, and the page states the block, how old it is, and the block this deployment began at, where every counter starts. A figure that could not be read says so in its place and is never shown as zero, and a rate with nothing to divide by, such as the default rate before any loan has opened, says there is none yet rather than 0%. If the latest re-read fails, the page says the figures are as of the block shown, not now. The page states that nothing on it comes from the app's indexer database.

Values in the reference currency use the protocol's own valuation rule, the one it applies to loan-to-value. The value on open loans and the lending totals since the start are valued now, not when each loan opened, and an asset the rule cannot value counts as nothing in them; so the page lists every asset ever lent with its own amounts and value at the same block, and names each asset a total leaves out. Interest counts what borrowers paid: collateral a lender received after a default is a recovery, not interest, and the page says so. NFT collateral has no price, so the page counts the open loans that carry it instead.

The fees section shows the treasury's share of interest and the loan start fee as set now, and the fees collected since the start, in the last day and in the last week, each valued when it was collected. Beside them it says a fee the protocol could not value then counts as nothing, and that the day and week figures are a lower bound once more fees than the scan limit fall in the window. The treasury is named and linked, and its custody is stated: when fees go to an outside account the protocol contract holds no fee balance, and only when the contract keeps the fees itself does the page show a held balance, saying it counts only assets of open loans. A treasury that differs from the app's deployment record is flagged. The page shows no price, return or buyback, and states the treasury is not invested to earn a return. Advances #3180.
