## Thread — history is assigned to eras once, by an operator under the pause, and the role freeze lifts (#1566 slice 4 PR C-5)

**Problem.** The era registry counts packets open for classification from the slice that introduced the count onward, and the per-day closure proof counts an era's closed days from the day it runs — but a deployment that was live before those existed holds packets nobody counted, in the live era or in one already retired. An era must not close over such a packet, and the role freeze that has protected the custody since activation ("value attributed, history not yet assigned") cannot be lifted until that history is placed.

**What changed.** A one-time, paused, operator backfill assigns history. Packets recorded before the count existed are not enumerable on-chain, so the operator supplies their hashes, page by page, from the recorded ingress events; each is placed in the era that was live at the time it was received — read from the registry's opening times, so no judgement is involved — and counted open there if unclassified value still stands under it, or marked counted and final if none does; a packet the ingress already counted is left alone. Completion records the count of history packets the operator attests against the number actually supplied — an acknowledgment, like the park's, recorded rather than proven on-chain — declares history assigned, and lifts the role freeze. From then on a custody credit no longer re-arms the freeze, because the carry-forward makes a role change safe; and every packet recorded from this slice on is appended to an on-chain list, so no later backfill needs an operator's list. Every backfill call states the pause epoch it was prepared under and is refused if a later pause has since begun.

**What is not needed.** No entry backfill: an era's outstanding reward liability is the per-day closure proof's count, and anyone may run that proof for a retired era's days.

**Spec:** TokenomicsTechSpec, slice-4 bullets gain a seventh change (the history backfill and the unfreeze).
