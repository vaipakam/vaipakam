## Website — plainer wording for offer assets held in the vault (#4042)

The website's whitepaper, the advanced user guide in every locale, and several landing-page and FAQ strings in German, French and Arabic now use plain wording for assets an offer sets aside in its creator's vault: "set aside in your vault" or "locked in your vault", matching what the app already says. When an NFT collateral listing sells, the guide now says the net proceeds are put in your vault.

The same plainer wording now covers the vault itself. Spanish and Chinese landing-page and FAQ strings that called the vault a "custody" now call it a vault, and the other locales, the English FAQ and the whitepaper's NFT-rental section say where an asset is held rather than calling the vault a custodian. Lines that state the Diamond itself holds funds for a step are kept as they are, because they say where the money actually is.

Nothing about how the platform behaves has changed.

Closes #4042.
