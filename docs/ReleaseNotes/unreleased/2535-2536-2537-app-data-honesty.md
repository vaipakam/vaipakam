## App — guided matching, Approvals, Vault and review receipts state what they know (PR #TBD)

**Guided matching recommends only offers the security check has answered for (#2535).** When a borrower or lender asks for matching offers, the app recommends up to five. Before this change, an offer whose token security check was still running showed with no badge at all, so an offer that would turn out to be flagged could sit in the list until the check finished. Offers past the first fifteen candidates were never checked and could be recommended unchecked. Now an offer whose check has not answered is shown in its place and marked "Checking", and it cannot be chosen until the answer arrives. If the check flags it, it drops out and the next offer moves up. Nothing beyond the checked part of the list is recommended. When flagged offers leave the list short, more of the list is checked, up to sixty offers. Past that, the list says more matching offers exist and points to the Offer Book, which shows every offer with its own badge. Accepting an offer was already blocked until its check passed; this change makes the recommendation itself honest.

**Approvals and the Vault say when a position list came from the backup source (#2536).** The standing approvals card in Settings and the Vault page build their token lists from the wallet's own loans and offers. Those lists are read from the chain, with the indexer as a backup. When only the backup answered, the lists could lag by a few minutes, and both surfaces still presented their results as complete. They now keep showing what they found and add a note that a token or asset from a position opened or received in the last few minutes may be missing. The note clears on the next refresh once the chain read answers.

**Review receipts show exact amounts (#2537).** The review receipt for accepting or posting an offer is what the user consents to. Its amounts were rounded for display to a few decimal places, sometimes upward, and very long figures lost digits, so the reviewed figure could differ from the one signed. The receipt now shows every amount (principal, interest and collateral) exactly as the transaction carries it. Lists and cards elsewhere keep the shorter rounded form, since nothing is being agreed to there. The other review surfaces (refinance, loan sale, offset, obligation transfer, early exit, the trading desk, rentals and VPFI) still round and are tracked in #2662.

The new copy is translated in the nine translated languages.
